## Full description (max 4000)

```
How many reels did you scroll today? Most people guess far too low.

ScrollTax counts every reel and short you scroll and adds up the time they take. A small brain counter sits on top of your feed, so the number is in front of you while you scroll, not hidden in a report you never open.

WHAT IT DOES
• Live counter: a small pill on top of your feed that goes up by one with every reel.
• Today at a glance: reels scrolled, total time and average time per reel.
• 7-day and 30-day charts, so you can see whether the habit is growing or shrinking.
• Month by month totals.
• A list of today's reels with the app, the time and how long each one held you.
• Home screen widget with today's count and time.

WORKS WITH
Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels, Snapchat Spotlight and the X video feed.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account and no sign-up.
• No internet permission. The app cannot send anything anywhere.
• No ads and no trackers.
• Only the app name, the time and the length of each reel are saved, on your phone.
• Delete your whole history any time from Settings.

HOW IT WORKS, AND WHY IT ASKS FOR ACCESSIBILITY ACCESS
ScrollTax uses the Android AccessibilityService API to notice when the reel or short on screen changes in the supported apps. It looks at on-screen information such as the position in the feed, the username or the title only to tell one reel from the next. That information is held in memory, never saved and never sent anywhere. The service is limited to the five supported apps. It performs no actions or gestures for you and does not record your screen.

ScrollTax also asks for permission to display over other apps. That is how the small counter is drawn on top of your feed.

A NOTE ON WHAT TO EXPECT
ScrollTax does not block anything and does not lock you out. It helps you notice how much you scroll, so you can decide what to do about it.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by Instagram, Meta, YouTube, Google, Snapchat or X.
```
