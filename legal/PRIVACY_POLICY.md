# ScrollTax Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

ScrollTax ("the app", "we") helps you notice how many short videos you scroll. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: **everything stays on your phone, and we collect nothing.**

## What the app does
ScrollTax counts the reels and shorts you scroll in Instagram, YouTube (Shorts), Facebook (Reels), Snapchat (Spotlight) and X (video feed), and how long you spend on each. It shows the count in a small overlay, in the app and in an optional home-screen widget.

## Information stored on your device
- The app name (for example "instagram"), the date and time, and the length of each reel you scrolled.
- Whether you accepted the disclosure.

This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not uploaded, not shared and not sold. We cannot see it.

## Accessibility service
ScrollTax uses Android's Accessibility service, and only for Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, Snapchat and X. It is used for one purpose: to detect that a reel or short has changed so it can be counted and timed. To tell one reel from the next it briefly reads on-screen text such as a username or title. This text is held in memory for a moment, never stored, never sent anywhere. The service does not read passwords, messages, payment details or any other app, does not take actions for you, and does not record the screen.

## Display over other apps
The overlay permission is used only to draw the small counter on top of the supported apps.

## What we do not do
- We do not collect personal data, contacts, location, photos, files or device identifiers.
- The app has no internet permission, no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs.
- We do not share or sell information.

## Your choices and deletion
- Turn off the Accessibility service or the overlay permission in Android Settings at any time. The app then stops counting.
- Use **Settings → Delete all my data** to erase your whole history immediately.
- Uninstalling the app removes all of its data.
- Your history is kept on your phone until you delete it.

## Children
ScrollTax is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for material changes, show a notice in the app.
